Company Background & Heritage

Engineering Solutions Built on Experience

Enock Construction & Earthmoving Equipment is a leading Bulawayo-based civil engineering and infrastructure contracting company. Established in 2010 (Company Registration No: BP 4/2010), we have spent over 15 years building the roads, bridges, township developments, and earthworks that underpin economic progress in Zimbabwe.

Starting with a dedicated core team and select earthmoving machinery, our steadfast commitment to engineering precision, strict timeline compliance, and client transparency has enabled us to grow into an industry leader possessing over 80 pieces of heavy machinery and employing over 200 skilled professionals.

Whether executing municipal road rehabilitation, high-density residential township infrastructure such as Mahatshula East and Valindre, or supplying heavy equipment to the mining sector, we deliver dependable quality backed by seasoned technical supervision.

Our Mission

To deliver enduring civil engineering and infrastructure solutions across Zimbabwe through superior technical expertise, dependable heavy equipment deployment, and adherence to the highest standards of safety, quality, and environmental stewardship.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted, capable, and preferred civil engineering and earthmoving partner in Southern Africa, recognized for transformative township infrastructure, resilient roadways, and dependable heavy equipment leasing.

Standards & Compliance

Health, Safety & Quality Management

At Enock Construction, safety is not merely a policy—it is an uncompromising operational value. We enforce comprehensive Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) protocols on every job site, ensuring zero harm to our personnel, subcontractors, and surrounding host communities.

  • Mandatory Daily Toolbox Briefings: Safety inspections before machinery operations start.
  • OEM Maintenance Standards: Strict preventive maintenance schedules on all 80+ fleet units.
  • Certified Operators: Valid licensing, medical certifications, and specialized heavy plant training.
  • Soil & Compaction Testing: On-site density testing and lab compaction verification.
