Road Construction & Rehabilitation

We provide comprehensive road engineering services from initial bush clearing and formation, sub-base preparation, roadbed stabilization, and aggregate compaction through to hot asphalt surfacing, bitumen chip seals, kerbing, and road marking. Our work complies with national transport standards and municipal road specifications.

Road Formation & Sub-base Asphalt Surfacing & Overlays Pothole Repairs & Resurfacing Precast Kerbing & Drainage

Service 03

Township Development

Enock Construction is Bulawayo's chosen infrastructure partner for modern residential developments, including the high-profile Mahatshula East and Valindre townships. We deliver complete turn-key infrastructure: surveying, arterial access roads, utility trenching, stormwater management, and fully serviced stands ready for housing construction.

Master Infrastructure Layout Residential Access Roadways Serviced Stand Preparation Stormwater Runoff Channels

Service 04

Earthworks & Bulk Excavation

With high-capacity CAT bulldozers, 30-ton excavators, front-end loaders, and heavy tipper trucks, we execute large-scale bulk earthmoving operations with speed and precision. Services include heavy site clearing, cut-and-fill slope balancing, deep foundation trenching, spoil removal, and engineered subgrade compaction.

Site Clearing & Grubbing Precision Cut & Fill Foundation & Trench Excavation Industrial Site Leveling

Heavy Equipment Hire

Our comprehensive leasing division offers equipment hire across Zimbabwe. Choose from wet hire (including experienced, certified machine operators and on-site maintenance) or dry hire. Motor Graders (CAT 140M / 12M) Heavy Excavators (CAT 320 / Volvo 360) Heavy Tipper Trucks (Mercedes / Hino) Compaction Rollers (Bomag / Dynapac)

Service 06

Asphalt & Paving Solutions

We operate mobile asphalt plant production capable of producing up to 80 tonnes per hour (80 TPH). We provide hot mix asphalt for highway overlays and heavy commercial haul roads, cold mix asphalt for instant road maintenance and pothole patching, mechanical asphalt paving, chip-and-spray bitumen seals, and interlocking concrete paving.

Hot Mix Asphalt Production Cold Mix Asphalt Bags & Bulk Mechanical Paver Surfacing Commercial Parking & Industrial Paving
