Enock Construction & Earthmoving Equipment

Engineering solutions built on experience.

Enock Construction & Earthmoving Equipment is a Bulawayo-based civil engineering and construction company specializing in infrastructure development, road construction, earthworks, and heavy equipment leasing.

With over 15 years of industry experience (Established 2010 | Reg. BP 4/2010), the company supports projects ranging from road rehabilitation and township infrastructure to large-scale earthmoving and equipment hire across Zimbabwe.

Our approach focuses on dependable delivery, quality workmanship, operational capability, and practical solutions for complex construction requirements.

  • Road construction and rehabilitation: Full formation, sub-base preparation, and asphalt overlays.
  • Civil engineering and public infrastructure: Stormwater drainage, water reticulation, and sewer lines.
  • Township development: Complete infrastructure for Mahatshula East, Valindre, and private developments.
  • Earthworks and bulk excavation: Site clearing, precision cut-and-fill, and industrial leveling.
  • Heavy machinery and equipment hire: Operated or bare leasing of over 80 fleet units.
